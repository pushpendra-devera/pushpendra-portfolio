## Overview

SeedCard is prior React Native commerce work covering product discovery,
advanced search and filtering, multilingual UI, cart management, checkout, and
Stripe payment integration.

## Contribution

The work focused on the connected customer journey: finding products, refining
results, maintaining cart state, completing checkout, and handling payment in a
cross-platform application.

This public entry intentionally avoids private customer information,
transaction records, client internals, and unsupported scale claims. It is
presented as representative delivery experience, not as a source-reviewed case
study.
