## Overview

FashionAggregator is the largest multi-role mobile product in this portfolio. I
maintained the private React Native project under accounts I own, with a single
application supporting buyers, sellers, personal stylists, and delivery
partners. Each role receives its own primary navigation and operational tools,
while authentication, notifications, messaging, profiles, localization, and
framework services remain shared.

## Buyer experience

The buyer journey covers campaign and category discovery, advanced search,
stores, product details, variants, wishlist, cart, saved addresses, checkout
entry, Tap payment screens, order status, returns and refunds, stylist
discovery, notifications, support, policies, and profile settings.

## Seller experience

Seller navigation is organized around a dashboard, catalogue, orders, stores,
and profile. The committed screens cover store creation and management,
catalogue maintenance, product details, inventory selection and CSV upload,
price updates, related-product pairing, promotions, incoming order decisions,
refund handling, earnings, and sales reporting.

## Stylist and delivery experiences

Stylists can create and verify profiles, manage portfolios, work with clients,
chat, receive styling requests, source products, create weekly plans, manage
orders, and select pricing plans. Delivery partners have onboarding and
document flows, vehicle management, delivery work, earnings, support, and
profile tools.
