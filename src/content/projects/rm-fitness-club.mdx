## Overview

RM Fitness Club is a production website for a real, two-location gym in
Musakhedi, Indore, India, built and launched end-to-end — site, domain, DNS,
email, analytics, SEO, and a custom bilingual exercise library — in a single
extended session, for an actual paying local business rather than a demo or
practice project.

## Context

The brief wasn't just "build a site." It included setting up infrastructure a
non-technical business owner couldn't do alone (domain DNS, email routing,
SSL), making licensing calls on a third-party exercise dataset before
publishing anything derived from it, and repeatedly choosing accuracy over
convenience — refusing invented stats and testimonials, correcting a
trainer-count mismatch, and re-checking the live site against the owner's own
printed flyer rather than trusting what was already published. The gym also
operates two physical locations, so contact mechanisms throughout the site
needed to route to the correct branch instead of assuming there was only one.
